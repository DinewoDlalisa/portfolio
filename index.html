About me

I came to design from the science lab, so I care about why something works, not only how it looks.

I hold an Honours degree in Microbiology and work as a Technology Transfer Officer, helping turn research into real-world innovation. Along the way I trained as a frontend software engineer and found my place where design meets code.

Alongside my day job, I take on UX/UI and graphic design gigs: interfaces, brand visuals and websites. I sketch and prototype in Figma, build in HTML, CSS and React, and use AI tools to explore ideas quickly before refining every detail by hand.

Through Enactus I worked on social entrepreneurship projects for local communities, and I still love work that solves real problems for real people.
